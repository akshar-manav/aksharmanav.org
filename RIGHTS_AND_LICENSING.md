# Rights and licensing boundary

Last reviewed: **2026-08-08**

This file explains what the repository's software licence does and does not cover. It is an operational policy, not legal advice. Rights ownership and any public licence for organisational material require confirmation by Akshar Manav and, where applicable, the individual creator.

## Software

Unless a file carries a different notice, original software created for this repository is licensed under the **GNU General Public License, version 2 or any later version** (`GPL-2.0-or-later`). The full terms are in `LICENSE`.

Software includes:

- custom Joomla templates and extensions;
- application code, scripts and automated checks;
- deployment definitions and infrastructure code;
- code examples intended to be executed.

This choice matches Joomla's GPL-2.0-or-later licence and avoids incompatible restrictions on Joomla-derived work. Third-party dependencies retain their own licences and must be reviewed before adoption.

## Material not covered by the software licence

The GPL software licence does **not** grant permission to reuse:

- organisational philosophy, articles, reports, website copy or project documents;
- Marathi, English or other translations;
- photographs, video, audio, illustrations, scans or archival material;
- the Akshar Manav name, logo, marks or visual identity;
- names, likenesses, quotations or other personality rights;
- personal data, participant records or private correspondence;
- third-party material included with separate permission.

Unless an item has an explicit rights notice, copyright and other rights remain with their respective holders and no general public reuse permission is granted.

## Per-item rights record

Before public use of content or media, record at least:

- title or stable asset identifier;
- creator and rights holder;
- source and creation/publication date when known;
- permission or licence and its evidence;
- allowed uses and required attribution;
- consent or privacy restrictions where people are identifiable;
- reviewer and review date.

Archive material must retain its date and context. A file being technically accessible does not establish permission to publish or relicense it.

## Translations

A translation may contain rights in both the source work and the translation. Do not apply an open content licence unless Akshar Manav has authority for the source work, the translator has agreed, and the intended licence has been approved. Machine assistance does not remove the need for rights and editorial review.

## Brand and personal data

No software or content licence grants trademark permission or implies endorsement. Personal data is governed by consent, privacy commitments and applicable law; it is not licensed merely because it appears in or passes through software.

## Contributions

By contributing original software, a contributor agrees that the contribution is provided under `GPL-2.0-or-later`. A contributor must not submit code they cannot license.

Content or media contributions require an explicit rights record. Do not assume that a code pull request is sufficient permission for organisational or creative material.

## Future open-content decision

Akshar Manav may later choose a Creative Commons licence for specified, rights-cleared material. Creative Commons licences are public and generally irrevocable once applied. That decision therefore requires a separate rights audit and explicit organisational approval; it is not made by this repository-wide software licence.
