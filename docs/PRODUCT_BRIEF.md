# Product brief v0.1

Status: **Draft for review**  
Product owner: **Ajinkya Kinhikar**  
Project: **aksharmanav.org**

## Product intent

Create Akshar Manav's authoritative public website and multilingual publishing platform. Phase 1 must be credible, deployable and extendible. It should explain the movement clearly, publish its work and ideas, help people participate, and establish durable foundations for a larger global platform.

Akshar Manav is understood here as a philosophy, a movement and an organisation. Its public presentation must preserve that depth.

## Organisational foundation

Akshar Manav's work began informally in 1981 under founder Rajan Khan. The organisation received formal government registration in 1996. Registration number `130007/96` appears in the available historical document and remains marked for documentary verification before legal presentation.

The philosophy centres on fundamental human identity and the possibility of people living in harmony with other people and nature. It seeks life free from corruption at every level through intrinsic understanding and conduct, rather than coercion. It works beyond divisions such as religion, caste, class, language, region, gender and borders.

## Phase 1 audiences

- people encountering Akshar Manav for the first time;
- existing participants and volunteers;
- writers, thinkers, organisers and practitioners;
- people seeking events, initiatives, publications or ways to join;
- translators, contributors and future local groups;
- search engines and AI systems seeking authoritative public information.

## Phase 1 outcomes

A visitor should be able to understand:

1. what Akshar Manav is;
2. the philosophy and way of working;
3. the distinction between the movement beginning in 1981 and formal registration in 1996;
4. current work, fields and events;
5. what Akshar Manav publishes;
6. how to participate or make contact.

## Phase 1 scope

- home;
- philosophy;
- about and history;
- how Akshar Manav works;
- fields and initiatives;
- multilingual publishing;
- events and announcements;
- join or participate;
- contact;
- Marathi and English editorial foundation;
- architecture for additional languages;
- accessibility, performance, security, privacy, SEO and AI-discoverability baseline;
- authentic photography, archival material, typography and original visual work.

## Publishing formats

The system should distinguish among essays and विचार, philosophy, literature, field reports, interviews, news, event reports, research or explainers, official statements, and documents. These should not be flattened into one generic blog stream.

## Explicit exclusions from Phase 1

- a full social network;
- automatic publication in dozens of unreviewed languages;
- donations before legal and financial review;
- complex member management;
- claims based only on the temporary website;
- page-builder lock-in;
- stock photography.

## Quality direction

The eventual identity and interface should aim for internationally excellent craft while remaining simple, accessible, recognisable across scripts, and appropriate for a serious intellectual and social movement. Visual design and logo decisions require a separate brand and design process.

## Success indicators for v1.0.0

- accepted core pages in Marathi and English;
- reliable editorial workflow;
- correct translation relationships and language metadata;
- accessibility and responsive checks passed;
- security and backup baseline verified;
- staging acceptance completed;
- production deployment and rollback verified;
- factual claims traceable to the truth register.
