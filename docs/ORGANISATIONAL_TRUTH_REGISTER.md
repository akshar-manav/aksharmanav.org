## Language rule for the two dates

Preferred factual construction:

> Akshar Manav's work began as an informal movement in 1981. The organisation was formally registered in 1996.

Do not describe 1981 as the registration year. Do not use 1996 in a way that erases the preceding fifteen years of work.

## Editorial handling

- Every material historical, legal, numerical or impact claim should have a traceable source.
- When evidence is incomplete, use qualified wording or withhold the claim.
- Archive material must retain date and context.
- AI-generated copy is always a draft and never becomes organisational truth merely through repetition.
