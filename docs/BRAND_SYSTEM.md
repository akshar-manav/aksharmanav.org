# Akshar Manav identity system

This document turns the supplied Akshar Manav mark into a practical digital system. It is an interpretation for the website, not a claim about undocumented historical intent. The organisation may refine the meanings as its custodians confirm them.

## Core idea

The mark contains eight independent coloured forms and one flame.

- The forms represent distinct people, identities, ideas and fields of work.
- Their colours remain individual; unity does not require sameness.
- The white space between forms represents room for dignity, dialogue and coexistence.
- Their shared arrangement creates relationship and collective direction.
- The flame represents living thought becoming action. It is not used as a religious or ceremonial symbol.

The organising phrase is: **स्वतंत्र ओळखी. सामूहिक दिशा. विचार कृतीत.**

## Logo usage

Use the supplied full-colour mark on paper-white or very light neutral backgrounds. On dark backgrounds, place the full-colour mark on a small paper-white field rather than recolouring it.

Do not:

- remove, add, reorder or merge forms;
- replace the white space with another colour;
- turn the flame into a generic decorative icon;
- scatter logo diamonds throughout every screen;
- use all logo colours simultaneously as interface decoration;
- redraw the symbol from memory.

The website SVG is a source-traced reconstruction from the supplied raster reference. It preserves eight forms, the flame and their measured relationships. A higher-resolution official master can replace it later without changing the system.

## Typography

The identity is Devanagari-first and editorial rather than corporate-dashboard-like.

- Display: Tiro Devanagari Marathi or Noto Serif Devanagari; Nirmala UI is the local fallback.
- Interface and body: Noto Sans Devanagari; Nirmala UI is the local fallback.
- Use large, short Marathi headlines with tight leading and generous white space.
- Keep body copy between 50 and 70 characters per line where possible.
- Never use more than two type families on one page.

Fonts are currently expressed as resilient system stacks so the local Joomla preview works offline. Production font files should be self-hosted after licensing and performance review.

## Layout and space

- Base content width: 86rem.
- Reading width: 48rem.
- Section space is deliberately generous: approximately 80–160px depending on viewport.
- Use asymmetry to create energy, then align text to a disciplined grid.
- Let white space perform the same role it has in the mark: separation without isolation.
- Cards are used only when content items truly behave as peers. Prefer open editorial rows for narrative material.

## Photography and illustration

Photography must be authentic to Akshar Manav, its people, places and work.

- Do not use AI-generated people.
- Do not use generic stock imagery to imply real participants or events.
- Identify location, event and approximate date in media metadata where possible.
- Use one strong image once, at a size that respects its detail.
- Black-and-white or restrained documentary treatment is welcome when the source supports it.
- When authentic photography is unavailable, use typography, white space and the real mark—never a synthetic scene.

The Maanus Sammelan 17 homepage image is the supplied photograph of Sitakhandi Wakad.

## Motion

Motion expresses people coming into relation; it must never turn the mark into confetti.

- The full mark may enter with a quiet scale, lift and rotation correction.
- Section content may reveal once as it enters the viewport.
- Navigation feedback should be immediate and restrained.
- Avoid perpetual movement, parallax and attention-seeking loops.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; all information must remain available without animation.

A future high-resolution motion master may animate the eight source forms assembling independently, provided their geometry and final spacing remain exact.

## Accessibility and responsive behaviour

- Text contrast must meet WCAG AA; yellow is not permitted for body text on white.
- Keyboard focus uses cyan and remains visible on every surface.
- Controls have a minimum practical target of about 44px.
- Mobile is single-column and does not depend on hover.
- Large headlines scale with the viewport and must not cause horizontal scrolling.
- Every meaningful photograph has Marathi alternative text.
- Decorative mark repetitions use empty alternative text.
- The provisional Google location must be labelled provisional until confirmed.
